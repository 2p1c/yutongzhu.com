But honestly, I don't have much say in this either. I've never controlled my diet, never looked into supplements, and never refined my form. I feel like most people are probably like that, right?

For me, working out no longer has any specific purpose. It's not about how many pounds to lose or how much weight to lift. I think people should just work out—it's already a part of my lifestyle. And I work out simply because I think having muscles is cooler and better looking; there's no special reason. Plus, it happens to be a healthy lifestyle that I can choose.

Working out doesn't need to be a miserable, bitter struggle. You don't need to punish yourself by running a few extra kilometers just because you ate a little high-calorie food. The chance to use your body's capabilities, and food—these are rewards that fitness brings us, not punishments for resting and enjoying food.

I really want to compete.
