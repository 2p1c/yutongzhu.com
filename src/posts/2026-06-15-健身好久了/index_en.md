But to be honest, I don't really have a say in this—I've never controlled my diet, never studied supplements, and never refined my form. I feel like most people are probably in the same boat.

For me, working out has lost its sense of purpose. It's not about losing a certain number of pounds or lifting a certain amount of weight. I just think people should exercise—it's already become a habit in my life. And honestly, I work out because I think muscles look cool and handsome; there's no special reason behind it. Besides, it's also a healthy lifestyle that I can choose.

Fitness doesn't have to be about suffering or punishing yourself with extra miles just because you ate something high-calorie. The chance to push your body, and food itself—these are the rewards fitness gives us, not penalties for resting or enjoying a meal.

I really want to compete someday.
