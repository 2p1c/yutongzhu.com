Which trip is this? I've lost count. Although we've only been to nearby small places, we will surely go abroad one day—out to the open sea, to the edge of the world, standing on the strangest rock or diving underwater. Driving ourselves is truly the best way to travel. It's not as tiring as walking, you can flexibly plan your itinerary, and you can stop to rest anytime. If there are few cars on the road and you're driving through such open landscapes, it feels great. You can keep food and drinks in the car and, more importantly, listen to music (someday we must buy a car with an excellent sound system), and you can bring along your loved one and friends.
